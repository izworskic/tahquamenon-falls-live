UPPER OR LOWER?

They are different trips.

UPPER FALLS

The big one.

Best first stop for the classic view. A paved approach and new accessible boardwalk lead toward the brink; the brewery and Fact Shack are in the same area.

Most dramaticAccessibleBrewery
LOWER FALLS

More to explore.

Smaller cascades around an island, an accessible boardwalk, the Olson Island Bridge, paddling access and the campground close by. Plan more time here.

UNDER THE HOOD

Why the live score is trustworthy.

01

Observed flow first

Actual USGS discharge drives the waterfall signal. Forecast rain never pretends to be water already over the falls.

02

Seasonal context

Today’s discharge is compared with historical USGS percentiles for this date, so September and spring runoff are judged differently.

03

Park-scale decisions

The tool answers the next question: Upper or Lower, hike or drive, brewery, campground, paddling or sunset.

04

Stale means stale

If the river gauge is old or unavailable, the site withholds the current score instead of manufacturing certainty.
