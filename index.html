WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

The waterfall engine, rebuilt for one place.

01

Observed flow first

Actual USGS discharge drives the waterfall signal. Rain in the forecast never masquerades as water already over the falls.

02

Seasonal context

The live discharge is compared against USGS daily percentiles for this date, so 500 cfs in September is not interpreted like 500 cfs during spring runoff.

03

Park, not pin

The output resolves the next decision: Upper or Lower, hike or drive, island, brewery, campground, paddling access or sunset at Rivermouth.

04

Failure is visible

Every live source has freshness and health. If USGS drops out, the site says so and lowers confidence rather than inventing certainty.
