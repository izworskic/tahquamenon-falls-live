Falls, lake and canyon stops.

Why can the falls look nearly dry?

The Snake River is heavily managed upstream for irrigation and reservoir operations. Late summer and fall can be minimal; spring snowmelt and release periods can be much larger.

When is Shoshone Falls usually strongest?

Spring is normally the strongest season. The City of Twin Falls says typical spring peaks can reach roughly 10,000–12,000 cfs, with wet years higher.

How much walking is required?

The closest parking is about 75 feet from the main viewing platform, and multiple viewpoints are ADA accessible.

Can I camp here?

No. Overnight camping is not allowed at Shoshone Falls or Dierkes Lake.
