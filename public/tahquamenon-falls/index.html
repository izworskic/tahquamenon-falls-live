    THE QUESTION ALMOST EVERYONE HAS

    Upper Falls or Lower Falls first?

    There is no universal answer. Start with the experience you would regret missing if time gets tight.

    UPPER FALLS

    Best default first stop.

    The signature waterfall is the fastest high-value stop. The paved approach, accessible boardwalk, multiple overlooks, Fact Shack and brewery make it efficient for a first visit.

    • Best when time is short
    • Best signature view
    • Strong accessibility option
    • Easy food pairing
    LOWER FALLS

    Best when you want to explore.

    Lower Falls is less about one big overlook and more about moving around: island access, boardwalks, multiple cascades, river access and nearby camping.

    • Better with kids who need movement
    • Island + bridge experience
    • More time-worthy wandering
    • Paddling and campground nearby

    WINTER IS NOT AN OFF-SEASON

    Tahquamenon is also a cross-country ski destination.

    Michigan DNR lists 4 miles of groomed cross-country ski trails in the Upper Falls area. The 1-mile Lantern Loop and 3.8-mile Giant Pines Loop use the park's hiking network, with backcountry skiing also allowed elsewhere in the park.

    PLAN BEFORE YOU LOSE A BAR OF SERVICE

    The practical Tahquamenon guide.

    Static answers for the questions people search before a trip, with the live planner above for the parts that change.

    How long do you need?

    45 minutes: make it an Upper Falls visit. 90 minutes: Upper Falls plus time for another overlook or food. About 3 hours: both falls without rushing. Half day: both falls plus the island, a longer walk or meal. Full day: add serious trail time, Rivermouth, photography or a neighboring destination.

    Accessibility

    Both falls have accessible viewing opportunities. The Upper Falls area now includes a 1,100-foot accessible boardwalk with switchback landings toward the brink. Lower Falls includes accessible viewpoints and the Ronald A. Olson Island Bridge. The park also lists track chairs at the Upper and Lower day-use areas.

    River Trail

    The point-to-point River Trail is about 5.1 miles between the falls. Roots, hills and stairs make it a real hike rather than a casual connector. Seasonal shuttle service can solve the one-way transportation problem, but verify the operating schedule before committing.

    With kids

    Do not assume the biggest waterfall is the whole day. Lower Falls gives children more to do: an island crossing, multiple cascades and room to move. For young kids, use Upper Falls as the quick signature stop and spend the larger share of time at Lower Falls.

    Food and breaks

    The Tahquamenon Falls Brewery & Pub is in the Upper Falls area, so it fits naturally before or after the main overlook. Lower Falls has seasonal concessions. The planner treats food as part of the route instead of sending you on a separate detour.

    Winter

    The park remains a real winter destination with groomed skiing, marked snowshoe trails, winter waterfall views and snowmobile access on designated routes. Use the XC conditions tool for regional snow intelligence, then verify park grooming locally before a long drive.

    QUICK ANSWERS

    Tahquamenon Falls FAQ

    Can I see both Upper and Lower Falls in one visit?

    Yes. They are about four road miles apart, and a three-hour visit is a practical baseline for seeing both without making every stop a sprint.

    Which falls is more impressive?

    Upper Falls is the park's signature single waterfall and the stronger choice when you have limited time. Lower Falls is a broader experience with multiple cascades, an island and more wandering.

    Do I need to hike between the falls?

    No. You can drive between the Upper and Lower day-use areas. The River Trail is an optional point-to-point hike, not the normal way most visitors have to connect the two.

    Is Tahquamenon worth visiting in winter?

    Yes. Winter adds frozen-waterfall scenery, groomed cross-country skiing, marked snowshoeing and a much quieter park experience. Winter access and grooming can change with conditions, so verify locally.
